FAQ

Do I need internet or an account?
No. The app runs entirely on your device, with no account.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
In your profile, create a backup and save the file, then restore it in the app on the new phone.

How do I share my routine with a friend?
From the routine screen, share it as a file, PDF, image or QR code.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app, or clear its data in your device settings. We hold no copy of your data.
